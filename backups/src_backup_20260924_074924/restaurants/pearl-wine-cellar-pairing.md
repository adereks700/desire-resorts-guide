A seated dinner inside Pearl's wine cellar, where the chef builds a menu around a wine you choose from the cellar itself rather than the other way around. It is one of the more intimate dining add-ons at Pearl and is typically arranged once you're on property rather than booked ahead of arrival. As with the beachside gazebo dinner, this generally comes with an additional charge on top of the all-inclusive rate. Confirm current pricing and availability with the resort, as offerings like this can change or be seasonal.
