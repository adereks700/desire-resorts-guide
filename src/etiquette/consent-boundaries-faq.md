<details class="accordion-item">
  <summary>Is clothing actually optional, or is there pressure to participate?</summary>
  <p>Optional means optional, no exceptions. You choose your comfort level in every space, every day, and that choice can change hour to hour.</p>
</details>
<details class="accordion-item">
  <summary>Will other guests or staff make assumptions if we do not participate?</summary>
  <p>No. Guests who stay in swimsuits all week and guests who spend it in the Jacuzzi Lounge are both just guests, staff treat every comfort level as the default.</p>
</details>
<details class="accordion-item">
  <summary>What does consent-forward mean here, day to day?</summary>
  <p>Observing is welcome, participating is invited (never assumed), and either can be declined at any moment without explanation.</p>
</details>
<details class="accordion-item">
  <summary>Can we watch without being expected to join in?</summary>
  <p>Yes. Spaces like the Jacuzzi Lounge are built for exactly that.</p>
</details>
<details class="accordion-item">
  <summary>What is the photography and recording policy?</summary>
  <p>Photos and video are welcome when everyone in the shot has said yes, which in practice usually means the privacy of your own room. Full policy details are available on our <a href='/legal/'>legal and privacy page</a>.</p>
</details>
<details class="accordion-item">
  <summary>How do staff enforce boundaries if something feels off?</summary>
  <p>Staff are trained to intervene immediately if a guest appears uncomfortable or a boundary is crossed.</p>
</details>
<details class="accordion-item">
  <summary>Do we have to decide our comfort level before we arrive?</summary>
  <p>No. Many couples arrive undecided and figure it out day by day.</p>
</details>
<details class="accordion-item">
  <summary>Is there a wrong way to experience Desire?</summary>
  <p>No. A couple that never leaves the beach loungers is having the exact intended vacation.</p>
</details>
