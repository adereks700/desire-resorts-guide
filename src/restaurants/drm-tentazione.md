Right at the pool, no need to towel off first. The menu runs from salads and ceviche to pizza and gourmet hot dogs and burgers, with vegetarian and gluten-free options available. Casual dress is the expectation (a cover-up or something on your feet), and walk-up service keeps it simple with no reservation needed. The separate poolside bar next door handles the drinks if you just want to sip something without eating.
