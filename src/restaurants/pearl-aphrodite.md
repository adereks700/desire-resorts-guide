Pearl's main dining room, facing the sea, with a full buffet, an à la carte menu, and hand-made wood-fired pizza all on offer across breakfast, lunch, and dinner, plus indoor and open-air seating depending on your mood. Dinner tends to bring a rotating theme most nights&mdash;Italian, Mexican, and other regional spreads are regulars&mdash;so it rarely feels like the same meal twice. The variety makes it a reasonably easy option for mixed tastes at the table. Casual dress works for breakfast and lunch; evenings lean a touch more polished, though it stays short of a strict dress code. No reservation is needed for the buffet.
