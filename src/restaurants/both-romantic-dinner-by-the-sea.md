A private, candlelit steak-or-lobster dinner set up in a beachside gazebo, away from the main restaurants, with the sound of the waves standing in for the usual background noise. It is offered at both Riviera Maya and Pearl and typically needs to be booked once you arrive, since setup is limited to a certain number of couples per night. Unlike the rest of the dining on this page, it generally comes with an additional charge on top of your all-inclusive stay. Confirm current pricing, availability, and booking lead time with the resort, as it can vary by season.
