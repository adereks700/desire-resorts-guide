Desire Riviera Maya's main buffet restaurant, with live cooking stations and a different theme most dinners so the spread rarely repeats itself&mdash;Caribbean, Italian, Mexican, and barbecue nights are regulars in the rotation. Seating spans both an indoor dining room and an open-air terrace, so you can catch the breeze or stay in the air conditioning depending on the evening. Casual dress works for both breakfast and dinner, and no reservation is needed. It's the easy, no-planning choice when you want variety and options for the table.
