A contemporary take on Mexican classics, with fresh ingredients and real craft behind the plating&mdash;it's the only restaurant on property built specifically around Mexican cuisine. Dinner is the main event and generally requires a reservation; dress is elegant casual. The adjacent Kahlo Cocktail Bar pours mezcal- and agave-forward drinks and is a good spot for a pre-dinner cocktail. Breakfast service is limited to Premier members when offered.
