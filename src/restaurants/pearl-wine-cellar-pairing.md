Branded on-site as CAVA, this is Pearl's dedicated wine spot&mdash;a charmingly intimate room for working through a list of international fine wines with help from the resort's sommelier. Beyond a glass by request, CAVA also runs special tastings where several couples sample and mingle together, a nice change of pace from a typical dinner reservation. It is one of the more distinctive add-ons at Pearl and is generally easiest to arrange once you're on property rather than booked ahead of arrival. Confirm current scheduling, format, and any additional cost with the resort, as tasting offerings like this can change or be seasonal.
