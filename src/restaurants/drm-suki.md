Sushi, sashimi, Thai specialties, and a teppanyaki table where the chef puts on a bit of a show. The regular à la carte menu is generally walk-in friendly, but the teppanyaki grill is a separate, reservation-only experience that books out quickly, so lock it in on check-in day if that's the draw&mdash;dinner service typically runs a fixed evening window, so arrive on time for your slot. Elegant dress is expected for dinner, the only seating offered.
