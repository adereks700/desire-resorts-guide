The most romantic table on property. An aphrodisiac-inspired menu, fine wine, and an intimate setting built for lingering over a long dinner, with decor built around eroticism and mystery&mdash;think dim lighting, suggestive artwork, and silhouette statues rather than anything overt. Reservations are required and dinner is the only seating offered. Elegant dress is expected; the adjacent Sahló Cocktail Bar is a good spot for a pre- or post-dinner drink while you wait for your table.
