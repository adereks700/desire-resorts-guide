A cozy coffee corner for real espresso and something fresh-baked, whether that's a slow morning start or an afternoon pick-me-up. It generally runs from morning into the early evening, with light late-night snacks available separately. Casual dress is fine and no reservation is needed&mdash;one of the easiest places on property to grab a quiet moment without leaving the resort rhythm.
