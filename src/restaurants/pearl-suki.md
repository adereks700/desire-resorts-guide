Fresh sushi and Thai specialties with an ocean view from both indoor and outdoor seating. Reserve ahead specifically for the teppanyaki table if that is the draw, since it tends to book out fastest; the regular à la carte menu is easier to get into. Casual dress is typical for dinner, the only seating offered; confirm current reservation windows when you check in.
