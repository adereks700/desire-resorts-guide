The Jacuzzi Room Ocean View pairs a Caribbean-facing outlook with a private four-person in-room jacuzzi. It is one of the more self-contained room options at Riviera Maya: you get the view, the jacuzzi, and a quieter setting than the suites clustered near the main pool. The resort also typically lists daily snacks, a docking station, a king-size bed, and welcome wine for this category. A strong choice if privacy and an ocean outlook matter more than being steps from the daily social scene.
