The Mansion Swim-Up Suites are ground-floor suites in the Mansion area with a private terrace leading directly into a swim-up pool. The resort also lists butler service, airport transfer, a dressing room, luxury bathroom, and premium liquor among the category's features. These are among the most social room placements at Pearl: you step from your terrace into the shared pool energy. A good match for couples who want both elevated amenities and the option to join the scene without walking far.
